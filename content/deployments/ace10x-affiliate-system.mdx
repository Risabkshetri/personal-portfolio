## The constraint

Ace10x runs on partners. People refer customers, those referrals convert, and
the partners get paid a commission. Somebody has to track all of that, and get
it right, because the moment a partner's numbers look wrong, they stop trusting
you.

The obvious answer is to buy an affiliate platform, and Ace10x had looked at
them. The problem was the fit. They were built for every business at once, so
Ace10x would be paying, month after month, for features it had no use for. The
tools we looked at were in the range of $50 to $100 a month, and that is before
you account for working around the parts that do not match how you operate.

So the question I took on was a narrower one than "build an affiliate platform":
what is the smallest system that does exactly what Ace10x needs, and can it live
inside the product they already have?

## What I built

I started by studying how affiliate software actually works, then cut it down to
the functions Ace10x depends on, and built those as a native module inside their
existing Django application, on PostgreSQL and Redis.

- **Referral and conversion tracking.** Every referral is recorded and tied to
  the partner who brought it.
- **A commission ledger.** Commissions are entries in a ledger, not a number
  recalculated on the fly, so any figure can be traced back to the events behind
  it.
- **Partner management and a partner dashboard.** Partners can see their own
  referrals and earnings without asking anyone.
- **Payouts and reporting.** The team can see performance and run payouts from
  the same place.
- **Fraud flagging.** Suspicious patterns get surfaced for a person to look at
  instead of being paid out silently.

Building it inside their application, rather than beside it, was a deliberate
choice. There is no second login, no sync job between two systems, and no
separate vendor to depend on.

## Outcome

The system is live in production. Referral tracking is running at 99% accuracy,
and Ace10x no longer needs a separate third-party platform for its core
affiliate workflows.

On the money: I will not claim a saving I cannot stand behind. The monthly fees
of the paid platforms are gone, but a system you own still has development,
hosting and maintenance behind it, so the real net figure depends on those.
What is not in doubt is that Ace10x owns the system and it fits how they work.
